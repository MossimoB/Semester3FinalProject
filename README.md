# StockSim

A JavaFX simulation of a fictional stock market. Start with $10,000 of virtual
money and try to reach $15,000 in ten simulated trading days by buying and
selling shares in six invented companies.

No real money, no real companies, no internet connection. Every price is
generated by a mathematical model written for this project.

install.

## How to play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `Space` | Play / pause |
| `S` | Cycle clock speed (1x → 2x → 4x → 10x) |
| `P` | Portfolio screen |
| `H` | High scores |
| `M` | Main menu |
| `Ctrl + S` | Save |
| `Esc` | Back one screen |
| `F1` | About — the maths, explained in-app |

The application opens on a loading screen that draws a generated market line
while it warms up, then the main menu.

The dashboard shows the six companies in a table, **all six price charts at
once** in a grid beneath it, and a large chart plus statistics for whichever
company is selected. Clicking a row outlines its small chart; clicking a small
chart selects the row.

## The price model

Once per simulated hour, each company moves by:

```
percentChange = trend + (volatility × Z)        where Z ~ N(0, 1)
newPrice      = currentPrice × (1 + percentChange)
```

`Z` comes from `Random.nextGaussian()`, so small moves are common and large
moves are rare. The change is proportional to the current price, so an expensive
company moves in bigger dollar amounts at the same volatility. A single tick is
clamped to ±25% and prices are floored at $1.00.

| Symbol | Company | Industry | Start | Volatility | Trend/hr |
|--------|---------|----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| NVT | NovaTech | Technology | $100.00 | 0.060 | +0.20% |
| GRG | GreenGrid | Renewable Energy | $75.00 | 0.040 | +0.15% |
| MDC | MedCore | Healthcare | $60.00 | 0.020 | +0.10% |
| AER | AeroWorks | Aerospace | $120.00 | 0.055 | −0.05% |
| FRB | FreshBite | Food | $40.00 | 0.020 | +0.08% |
| QAI | QuantumAI | Artificial Intelligence | $150.00 | 0.100 | +0.30% |

AeroWorks declines on purpose. Without a company that loses money over time,
"buy everything and wait" wins and the game contains no decisions.

### Volatility drag

Measured over 3,000 simulated games by `demo/BalanceCheck`:

```
SYM   COMPANY            MEAN     MEDIAN        P10        P90
QAI   QuantumAI       +23.90%    -15.25%    -73.20%   +151.22%
```

QuantumAI has the **highest trend of any company** and the **worst median
result**. That is not a bug:

```
A stock gains 20%, then loses 20%:
    $100 × 1.20 = $120
    $120 × 0.80 =  $96      → down 4%
```

A 20% loss needs a 25% gain to undo. The more violently a price swings, the more
this compounds against it. Over 80 ticks at 10% volatility the drag is roughly
`σ²/2 × n = 0.40` in log terms — enough to swamp the trend advantage.

A few players get rich on QuantumAI and most lose money on it. That is how a
real high-risk asset behaves, and the model was never told to do it.

## Save format

Saves go to `~/.stocksim/savegame.json`. They store the **seed** and the **tick
count**, not the prices.

Loading rebuilds the game from the seed and ticks it forward. Because the price
generator is seeded and the event generator shares its randomness, that
reproduces the market exactly — same prices, same full history, same news, same
order. Only cash, holdings, and trade history are stored, because those came
from the player rather than the maths.
